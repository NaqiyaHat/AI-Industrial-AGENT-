# Centrifugal Pump Etanorm 65-200 (P-101, P-102) - Maintenance Notes

## Bearing temperature limits
Normal drive-end bearing temperature is 55-70 degC at full load with 25 degC ambient.
Raise a warning above 75 degC and stop the pump above 85 degC. A sudden rise of more than
6 degC above the normal running temperature indicates a lubrication or alignment problem,
even if the absolute limit has not been reached.

## Bearing overheating - troubleshooting
1. Check grease condition and quantity. Too much grease causes churning and heat just as
   too little grease causes friction. Correct fill is 30-50% of the free bearing space.
2. Check that the correct grease is used: SKF LGMT 2 or equivalent lithium grease.
3. Measure vibration on the bearing housing. Above 4.5 mm/s RMS suspect misalignment or wear.
4. Check motor-pump alignment with a laser tool; tolerance 0.05 mm offset.
5. Check the pump is not running far from its best efficiency point (throttled discharge).

## Regreasing procedure
1. Clean the grease nipple. 2. Remove the drain plug. 3. Inject 15 g of LGMT 2 with the pump
running. 4. Run for 30 minutes with the drain open so excess grease can escape. 5. Refit the
plug. Interval: every 2,000 operating hours or 3 months.

## Bearing replacement procedure
1. Lock out / tag out the motor and close suction and discharge valves.
2. Remove the coupling guard and coupling spacer.
3. Remove the bearing bracket; press out the shaft with the bearings.
4. Heat new 6309-2Z bearings to 100 degC with an induction heater and fit them; never hammer.
5. Reassemble, align the coupling with a laser tool and regrease.
6. Run for 1 hour and confirm bearing temperature below 70 degC and vibration below 2.8 mm/s.
Parts: 2x SKF 6309-2Z bearing. Typical time: 3 hours, 2 technicians.

## Mechanical seal replacement
Replace the seal kit KSB-SEAL-65 when leakage exceeds 10 drops per minute. Never run the
seal dry. Typical time: 2.5 hours.
