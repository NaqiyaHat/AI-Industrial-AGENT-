# Screw Air Compressor GA 37 (COMP-201) - Maintenance Notes

## Service intervals
Intake filter: every 4,000 hours or when the filter differential pressure exceeds 50 mbar.
Oil and oil filter: every 8,000 hours, or sooner if oil analysis shows oxidation.

## High element temperature
If the element outlet temperature exceeds 110 degC the compressor shuts down. Check the
cooler for blockage, the oil level, the thermostatic valve and the ambient temperature
(maximum 46 degC).

## Intake filter replacement
Stop and depressurise the compressor. Open the filter housing, replace element AC-FILT-GA37,
check the seal, close and reset the service counter.
